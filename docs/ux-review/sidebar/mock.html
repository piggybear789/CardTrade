Catalog sidebar: four directions

Today the sidebar is four nav rows and three filters behind "›" with "Any", over a lot of empty space, and no card games at all. Alt and Fanatics stand out because the sidebar is the catalog itself: categories with art and counts, filters you can use without opening anything. References: Etsy and Zendesk (categories with counts), Klarna, Faire and Behance (open filter groups, price slider with inputs, checkboxes with counts), Care.com (pill choices), Databricks and fal (dense checklists). All four keep Sell an item at the top.

A · Games first, filters open (recommended)
The card games are the top-level navigation, each with its logo and live count, like Alt's categories. Under them every filter is already open: condition as pills, price as a slider with two boxes, a "Sold" toggle. One tap per filter, nothing hidden behind "›".
