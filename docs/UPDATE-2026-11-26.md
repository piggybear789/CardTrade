## New: bank transfers for US sales

A US buyer on a USD sale can now pay by **bank transfer** as well as by card:

1. The buyer chooses **Bank transfer** in the Pay dialog.
2. NoDitto shows the account to send to (account name, routing number, account
   number, bank) and a reference, each with a copy button, plus a link to Stripe's
   printable instructions.
3. The buyer sends the money from their own bank. ACH usually arrives within a business
   day and a wire the same day. The card stays reserved meanwhile.
4. When the money arrives, the sale moves to **payment held**, exactly like a card
   payment, and continues as normal (ship, inspect, payout).

How it handles the awkward cases:

- **The buyer can't pull the money back.** It's a payment they send, not a debit, so
  there's no dispute or chargeback process. That's why it's cheap and safe.
- **Nothing arrives:** after **72 hours** the sale is cancelled and the card goes back
  on the catalog.
- **Part of the money arrives:** the transfer details show the amount still owed. If it's
  cancelled after part of it arrived, that part is refunded to the buyer's bank.
- **Refunds** go back to the buyer's bank. Stripe emails the buyer for their bank
  details if it needs them, and the buyer has 45 days to reply.
- **Only USD sales, and only US buyers.** Stripe doesn't support bank transfers on
  sales charged on behalf of a seller, and every non-USD sale is charged that way. It
  isn't available in Australia at all.

The sale room's button reads **View transfer details** while a transfer is pending, so
the buyer can reopen the details on any device.

- The buyer pays less than half the card fee, and NoDitto keeps about the same amount as
  on card from about US$150 up.
- **"Stripe (est.)" for bank transfers is an estimate.** Stripe's test mode charges US$0.00
  on these payments, and I couldn't read the live price. I set the estimate high on
  purpose: 0.8% processing plus the usual payout cost (0.25% + about US$0.25). **After the
  first live transfer, check its fee in the Stripe Dashboard** and the cost table
  (`STRIPE_BANK_COSTS` in `domain/fees/processingFee.ts`) can be tightened.
- The negative rows come from the decision to drop the fixed "+ $0.60" part, below.
  NoDitto absorbs Stripe's fixed per-sale cost on small sales.

## Fees: no fixed part any more

This work was half-done in the working copy when I started (in another session). I
finished it so the tests pass.

- Every rate is now a plain percentage, with no "+ A$0.60". For example 3% by an
  Australian card, 4.5% by a US card, 2.5% PayTo, 1.5% iDEAL and 2% bank transfer.
- Copy now says "NoDitto keeps about 1% on larger sales, less on small ones".
- NoDitto absorbs Stripe's fixed fee (about A$/US$0.55 a sale) as a deliberate cost of
  winning buyers. The percentage covers it from about A$50 / US$41 up; below that,
  NoDitto loses a few cents to about 30 cents a sale.
- The other session also updated the Terms, Privacy, Safety and product steering wording,
  plus the Flutter app's copy of the rates (`flutter_app/lib/core/money.dart`).

## UI changes

- **The "your move" strip** in the sale and trade rooms:
  - one row, with the step's short title on the left and the button on the right
  - no "Your move" pill
  - the explanation behind an (i) next to the title
  - at most one button, with the rest in the ⋯ menu
  - the title wraps to two lines at most, never cut off
  - every step has a short title written for this space ("Handover terms",
    "Waiting on payment")
- **The Pay dialog:**
  - the icons and radio buttons are centred
  - the per-option fee line is gone (it's in the breakdown below)
  - totals no longer run off the edge on phones
  - Bank transfer is a new option for US buyers
- **The Buy dialog's card row** is one line tall.
- **Listings show suburb and state only**, never a street address ("Sydney NSW", not
  "12 Test Street, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia").
- **The fulfilment option that wasn't chosen** uses a dashed edge instead of 60% opacity,
  which failed the contrast check.

## Fixes

- **A card hold could stay live on a cancelled trade.** If a trade was cancelled while
  its holds were being placed, the hold placed in that gap was never released. Placement
  now checks the trade again afterwards and releases its holds if it has ended
  (`lib/trades/collateralPlacement.ts`).
