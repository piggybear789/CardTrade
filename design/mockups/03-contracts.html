What changes

  1. 1
    B4 The header carries a status badge, and the tones become semantic. The cash-sale header passes no status today while the trade header passes one — same component, two contracts. And CANCELLED renders outline here but secondary in the trade room. "Inspecting" now uses the filled-iris "money is held" tone, which is what distinguishes it from "In transit" — currently both are the same solid chip. See P3.
  2. 2
    B2 InspectionCountdown moves out of the Protection tab and becomes a banner. This is not a new component or new copy — it is the existing one, rendered where the trade room already renders it. Today a buyer has to open the third tab of the inspector to discover that a clock is running which will pay the seller without them. Same component, same strings, one placement instead of two.
  3. 3
    B1 The dock title speaks to the reader, and names the deadline. This is the change you were asking about, and the answer is that the sentence exists but the title does not. deriveCashSaleSteps emits label: 'Buyer confirms the item arrived' — and that label is the dock's <h3>, so the buyer reads a third-person description of themselves as the largest text in the card. The detail beneath it is in the right voice, but it is hidden below md. Fixing the labels fixes the rail captions too, since both read the same field.
    Voice is also inconsistent across steps as written: "Set handover terms" is imperative, "Payment collected and held" is passive, "Buyer confirms the item arrived" is third person. One voice — second-person imperative — for all of them.
  4. 4
    B2 On mobile the countdown is the one thing that must not be behind the sheet. Same fix, and this is where it matters: the phone room is the chat, so every contract fact lives behind a docked sheet. A deadline with an automatic financial outcome cannot be one of them.
  5. 5
    B1 The phone dock gets the deadline on its second line. The detail line is hidden below md by design, which is a reasonable space decision — but it means the phone shows the least informative half. A short deadline string fits the same budget.
  6. —
    KEEP Everything structural. The flush shell, the 3fr/2fr split with a 24rem chat floor, panes that scroll internally rather than a scrolling document, the tab inspector and its action slot, the dock living in the chat column, the bottom sheet on mobile with its "Done" button and no ✕, the fee restated in the pay dialog, and absolute rather than relative timestamps.
