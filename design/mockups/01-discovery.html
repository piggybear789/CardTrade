What changes

  1. 1
    ADD The price ladder gains a histogram and two typed fields. The non-linear ladder is the right mechanic — each drag stays proportionate to the price it lands on — but it hides two things. A member cannot see where the inventory actually sits, and cannot enter $500 if the nearest stop is $400. The histogram answers the first, the fields answer the second, and the ladder still owns the drag and still owns the URL. Ref: Klarna — histogram over a dual slider with min/max fields.
  2. 2
    CHANGE The result count moves onto the heading line. Below the h2 it reads as a subtitle; beside it, it reads as the size of what you are looking at. Refs: Urban Outfitters, lululemon — count inline with the category title.
  3. —
    KEEP Everything else, as built. The unlabelled rail panel, Condition behind a closed accordion with its count badge, no applied-filter chips, no facet counts, both search fields, the near-black selected chip, two separate availability toggles, the mosaic on mobile, the bottom-sheet filters, infinite scroll on mobile against pagination on desktop, and no create CTA in the phone chrome.
  4. —
    NOTE Prices are ink here, not violet. That is the P2 palette change, and the catalog is where it does most of its work — a four-column grid was previously showing violet as the loudest colour on the page. See P2.

What changes

  1. 1
    ADD A vertical thumbnail filmstrip on the stage gallery. Nine photos are currently reachable only through a small « 1/9 » pill or a drag. For a graded card the photo set is the evidence, and a buyer wants to jump straight to the back or the slab label rather than page through eight images to reach it. Ref: Etsy.
  2. 2
    ADD One line for the fee, beside the price. The full "what you'll pay" card is dropped — this is the smallest thing that stops the 5% first appearing in the contract room's Payment tab, which on a phone is behind a bottom sheet. A buyer can currently reach the pay confirmation having only ever seen $1,850 when the charge is $1,942.50. The dialog does restate it, and that is a real mitigation, but one clause here costs nothing.
  3. 3
    CHANGE The inline map becomes a meta line. "Single item · Listed 4 hours ago · Based in Newtown NSW" carries the three facts the map was standing in for, in one line instead of a 224px static image — and listing age, which the map never showed and which every resale reference puts on the page. Refs: Depop "LISTED 2 DAYS AGO"; Nextdoor "15h · 1.4 mi · Cambridge".
  4. 4
    KEEP The action stack exactly as built. ProposeTradeDialog, BuyButton, MakeOfferDialog, MessageSellerButton, in that order, pinned to the bottom of the pane. Round 1 proposed reordering and relabelling these; withdrawn.
  5. 5
    CHANGE The photo stack becomes a left-to-right carousel. appearance="stack" renders one full-width frame per photo at natural aspect down the page, so a nine-photo listing puts a very long scroll between the description and anything after it. One frame, swiped, with a counter, dots and a peek at the next image so the gesture is discoverable. Heights still come from stored image_dims, so nothing jumps. Ref: eBay's item gallery — single frame, horizontal swipe, counter.
  6. 6
    KEEP ListingBuyerBar as built — message, heart, icon-only Offer and Trade squares, then Buy as the only filled control. Round 1 argued the icon-only money buttons ask a lot of a first-time member; withdrawn.
  7. —
    CUT The trust strip and the condition-report card are gone. Both were round-1 additions and both are over-explaining on a page whose job is to show a card and a price.

What changes

  1. 1
    ADD Four exits instead of one. There is currently a single submit, no cancel and no draft, so a seller part-way through an $1,850 listing has nowhere to put it and dismissal is the browser back button with unsaved work. Ref: Etsy Shop Manager — Cancel / Preview / Save as draft / Publish.

    ADD Named photo slots in a deliberate order. For a graded or near-mint card the back, corners and grading label are the valuation, and their absence is what a "not as described" dispute turns on. Naming them also gives arbitration something to check against. Ref: Depop.
  3. 3
    ADD Echo the derived title. deriveItemTitle() takes the first line of the description and this is deliberately not narrated. But that title is what appears in every grid, contract and notification, so a seller who opens with a paragraph gets a title they never chose and cannot see. One line, no new field.

What changes

  1. 1
    CHANGE Columns are declared on the container, so every cell aligns down the page. This was the review note, and it is a real structural point rather than a tweak: listrow is flex, so each row sizes its own cells from its own content and the price in row 1 lands at a different x than the price in row 2. Declaring grid-template-columns once — thumbnail, listing, price, watching, status, actions — makes the alignment a property of the table rather than a coincidence. Status also gets its own centred column, so badges of different widths still stack.
  2. 2
    ADD Watchlist filter chips and change markers. Twelve saved cards with no way to see which changed is a list you stop opening. Price history and watch_count are already on the row, so nothing new is needed server-side. Sold saves stay, greyed and labelled "for reference" — that is the comparable-sales use the "include sold" filter exists for.
  3. —
    KEEP Saved stays a browse grid. It is a browse surface, so a shopper's grid is right there — only My listings becomes a management table.

Seller profile

/sellers/[id]

Round 1 put trust facts in a right rail beside the listings grid. The review was right that it looked arbitrary — and the reason is structural, not cosmetic: two columns of unrelated cards have independent content lengths, so their heights cannot align. The fix is to stop having two columns. One band, full width, one row of facts, equal by construction.

What changes

  1. 1
    CHANGE One full-width trust band instead of two rails. The band is a single grid of auto-fit facts, so it is one object with one height and it reflows to three columns at tablet and two on a phone without ever leaving a ragged edge beside something else. It also merges what were two separate cards — the identity disclosure and the track record — because they answer one question. "Disputes upheld: 0" is the most decision-relevant number we hold and is currently shown nowhere.
  2. 2
    CHANGE Listings / Reviews / Recently sold become tabs. Reviews are currently a second section below the grid, reached by scrolling past however many listings the seller has, and sold history is not on the page at all — despite being the best evidence that a seller actually ships.
  3. 3
    CHANGE On a phone the band collapses to one sentence. Six facts as a grid at 390px is six lines a buyer skims; one sentence carrying name, method, date, region, volume and dispute count gets read.

    KEEP The bio outside the verified block — member-authored text must never sit inside a provider-verified frame — and labelled rather than icon-only verification.
