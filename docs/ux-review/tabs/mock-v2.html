Detail tabs, round 2: hierarchy, Exchange and History

Terms and Collateral are kept as approved. This round looks at why the tabs feel empty, then gives two takes each on Exchange and History. Same trade, at Inspect, seen by Alice.

1 · The whitespace problem

The tabs are not too loose inside; they are too short for the box they sit in. The details pane is as tall as the chat beside it (about 680px), and three of the four tabs need 250 to 400px, so each one ends in a large blank area. Two ways out, compared:

A · Keep tabs, size them to their content
The pane shrinks to the tab and the page background shows below it. Simple, but the pane changes height on every tab switch, which the room's no-jump rule forbids.

All four sections stacked in one column, in the order a member asks: what is being swapped, where and when, what is on hold, what has happened. The tabs become jump chips that scroll to a section. No blank space, nothing hidden behind a click, and one fixed height.

C · Keep tabs, fill the space with the next useful thing
Each tab ends with a related block: Exchange with "What happens next", Terms with "Safety at the meeting", and so on. Uses the space, but it is filler that pushes the useful parts apart.

Exchange, then a "What happens next" block
Hierarchy, whichever way
  • Every section opens with its answer in one line (what's swapped, where, how much is held), detail after.
  • Labels are sentence-case, small and grey; values are the dark, bold thing. Today the caps labels ("VALUE", "HOW IT ENDS") are louder than some values.
  • One size for money in lists; a big figure only where it IS the answer (the hold).
  • Spacing is 12px inside a section and a hairline between sections, rather than cards inside cards.
  • Nothing the header or the next-step row already says is repeated (names, ID badges, status).

2 · Exchange and History, two takes each

Exchange (E1, E2): round 1 made the swap clear but kept two boxes and a separate fee list; these are tighter. History (H1, H2): two ways to make it a timeline worth opening.

E1 · One ledger (recommended)
One box, two rows: what goes, what comes, each with its condition and value, the ⇅ between. Fee and the frozen-terms note as one footer line. About half the height of round 1.
