Showing the steps: six other ways

All six sit inside option A's status card: Bob on the first line, the card under it. Same trade, at Inspect with 1 day of the 3-day window left. Sources: eBay's and SHEIN's order steppers, Talabat's arrival ring, Temu's "Waiting to pick" time bar, Airbnb-style step chips, and Uber Eats' expandable timeline.

1 · Dots and lines
The classic order tracker (eBay "Paid → Tracking → Delivered", SHEIN). Each step a dot, done steps filled, the current one ringed. Reads at a glance; four labels fit at 360px.

2 · Countdown ring (recommended)
The ring is the deadline, not the steps: it empties as the window runs out (Talabat, Uber). The steps become one line of text. The thing you act on, time, gets the picture.

3 · Time bar
Temu's "waiting to pick" bar: the bar is how much of the inspection window is used, labelled with start and end. Honest about time, but a bar per step means it resets every step.

4 · Step chips
A row of pills: done ones grey with a tick, the current one filled violet, the rest dashed. Names every step in full, so it scales to six steps by scrolling sideways.
