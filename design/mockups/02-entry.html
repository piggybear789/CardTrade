What changes

  1. 1
    ADD An editorial half — reduced to two lines. Round 1 had a three-bullet feature list here; that is the over-explaining the review flagged. Headline, one sentence, one stat. A centred 360px form on a 1280px screen otherwise spends 70% of the viewport on background. Ref: Etsy split-screen.
  2. 2
    KEEP CHANGE Keep the distinct error codes; give the unconfirmed-email case two exits. Separating EMAIL_NOT_CONFIRMED from INVALID_CREDENTIALS is already better than most products manage. What it lacks is resend and correct-the-address, so a member who mistyped at sign-up currently gets an accurate diagnosis and no way out.
  3. 3
    CUT The escrow explainer card is gone. "Escrow on every purchase. Sellers are ID-verified. Money is held until you accept." — removed from the mobile form and not restated in the desktop column. A returning member does not need the pitch twice on one screen.
  4. —
    ADD "Browse without an account" on both breakpoints. Guests can browse and the middleware allows it, but nothing on the auth screens says so, so the wall reads as mandatory.

What changes

  1. 1
    ADD Show the runway and mark what each step unlocks. The two-gate model is good design — verify who you are quickly, collect bank details only when money has to move — but a member currently discovers it one refusal at a time. Refs: Fiverr; Etsy.
  2. 2
    ADD Explain the stricter email rule. Sign-up validates harder than sign-in because this address becomes the payment-provider contact — a real reason the member cannot guess. Without it a rejected address reads as a bug.
  3. 3
    ADD Live password requirements. Rules revealed only by rejection are the most common avoidable sign-up failure.
  4. 4
    CHANGE DUPLICATE_ACCOUNT gets its two exits. The code exists; the state should offer sign-in and reset rather than only naming the problem.

Notes

  1. 1
    KEEP Everything on this screen, as built. The pill dots, the always-open dialog, the region step being shown even when only one region trades (silently assigning it would make the later "your region is tied to your payout account" refusal come from nowhere), the catalog escape on every step, and the seller handoff sitting off the end of the count.
  2. 2
    B3 The counter never says "1". PROGRESS_STEPS includes welcome — so the count is out of 4 — but the dot row is hidden on welcome. The first number a member ever sees is "Step 2 of 4", and "4 of 4" lands on a screen that is followed by more work for sellers. Dropping welcome from the count makes it read 1–2–3 of 3.
    Round 1 also called the seller step's missing progress a bug. It isn't — the array's own comment gives the reason, and it is correct: counting a seller-only step would tell a buyer they are on "step 4 of 5" of something they will never see. If that step needs progress it should have its own, for identity and payouts.
