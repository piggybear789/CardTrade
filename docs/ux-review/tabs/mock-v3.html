Filling the pane: one stretchy part per tab

Yes. Keep the pane at the chat's height (so nothing jumps), and give each tab ONE part that grows into whatever height is left, marked with a dashed outline here. Everything else stays its natural size, packed at the top. On a short screen the stretchy part shrinks first, and the tab only scrolls when even that runs out. Drawn at the real pane size, 352 × 680.
