One "Summary" tab for every contract room

The same pattern for trades and cash sales: the first tab is a fact sheet. Each fact is one labelled row (label on the left, answer on the right), photos and the map shrink to thumbnails beside the text, and anything long (the full map, postage tracking, the payment breakdown, evidence) lives in its own tab. A whole contract fits in about 380px, so the tab ends where the facts end instead of trailing into a big map or blank space.
