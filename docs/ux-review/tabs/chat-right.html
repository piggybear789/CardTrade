Chat on the right: the contract becomes the page

Flip the room. The contract is the main area, a page you read top to bottom; the chat is a fixed 380px panel down the right side, as in PandaDoc, Spellbook, ClickUp, Turo and Airbnb. The main area is wide, so sections can sit side by side instead of stacking into a tall thin column, which is what made every earlier version feel empty or cramped. Three ways to lay out that page, all at 1280 × 720.

1 · Overview grid (recommended)
A status band across the top, then the four sections as a 2 × 2 grid of tiles that fill the area. Everything is visible at once, with no tabs and no scrolling on a normal laptop. Like a sweetgreen or Turo order page with the chat beside it.
